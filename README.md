# Wardrobe AI

A personal AI stylist that builds outfits only from the clothes you already own.

- **Wardrobe**: photograph your pieces, organised into Tops, Bottoms, Outerwear, Shoes, Accessories, Dresses and Other.
- **Outfits**: a lookbook of complete looks for the occasion, season and weather.
- **Stylist**: ask in your own words and get looks from your wardrobe.

Live site: https://vanyaforbes.github.io/wardrobe-ai
